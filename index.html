Intro

Nobody starts with the right answer. You start with fifty wrong ones.

Scribbled fast, half of them ugly, most of them fine. Somewhere in that pile is the mark — the one that still works upside down, tiny, or glanced at from across a room.

Finding it, cutting it down, and shipping it before the deadline eats you alive. That's the whole job. We're just fast at it.
